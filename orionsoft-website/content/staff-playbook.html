How we work in the Staff Office

Our office is online. This playbook explains the order of the company, what each department does day to day, how every workflow runs in the Staff Office, and exactly how performance is measured, so everyone works to the same standard.

Sign in: orionsoftlimited.com/staff Working hours: from 09:00 WAT Weekly report: every Friday
01 · Start here

How to use this guide

Everyone reads sections 1 to 5 and 7 to 10. Then read your own department in section 6, and the departments you work closely with.

Two labels appear throughout:

  • System rule is enforced or measured by the Staff Office. It affects your records and your score automatically.
  • Recommended practice is how we expect you to work. Your manager reviews it, but the system doesn't enforce it.

Before your first day

  1. Sign in at orionsoftlimited.com/staff with the email and password HR gave you. Tick Keep me signed in on your own phone. You can also link your Google account in My Profile and use Continue with Google afterwards.
  2. Complete your profile: photo, phone, WhatsApp number, short bio and social links. Colleagues use it to find and contact you.
  3. Set up your phone in Field Visits → Set up your phone: test location, test camera, and turn on notifications. Do this even if you work in the office; location checks and alerts depend on it.
  4. Install the app: in Chrome tap Install, or on iPhone tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  5. Read the Handbook & Links page and your department section below.

03 · Rhythm

Daily, weekly and monthly rhythm

Every working day

Start

  • Clock in from the Lobby by 09:00 (15 min grace).
  • Choose your mode: office, remote, field or client site.
  • Post your stand-up: yesterday, today, blockers.
During the day

Work

  • Move tasks through To do → In progress → Review.
  • Check in and out of every client visit.
  • Answer any location check within 20 min.
  • Reply to messages and meeting invites.
End of day

Close

  • Update task status and goal progress.
  • Clock out with a short end-of-day note.
  • Forgotten clock-outs are closed automatically and flagged.
Every Friday

Weekly report

  • Submit your weekly report (reminder sent on Fridays).
  • Managers review reports on Friday or Monday.
Monthly

Performance

  • Check your scorecard in Performance.
  • Managers review team scorecards and flags with each person.
Automatic

Reminders you'll get

  • Overdue tasks and goals due soon.
  • Deal and stakeholder follow-ups due today.
  • Meetings: morning digest and 15 min before.
  • Birthdays and work anniversaries on the feed.

System rule Work start time, grace period and the location-check window are set by management in Office settings. The defaults are 09:00, 15 minutes and 20 minutes.

04 · Everyone

Core workflows

These apply to every department.

Clock in and out

  1. Open the Lobby, choose your work mode and tap Start my day.
  2. Allow location when asked. Your location is recorded only at clock-in, clock-out, client check-ins and location checks, never in the background.
  3. If the phone has no signal, tap Clock in now (location added when found). Keep the Staff Office open for a few minutes and it attaches your location.
  4. Post your stand-up when prompted.
  5. At the end of the day tap End my day and write a short note.

System rule Arriving after the start time plus grace counts as late. Late arrivals of 30 min or more notify your manager.

Tasks

StatusMeaningWho moves it
To doAssigned, not startedCreated by your lead or yourself
In progressYou're working on itYou
ReviewFinished, waiting for a checkYou
DoneAcceptedYour lead (or you, for your own tasks)

Priorities are low, medium, high and urgent. System rule Tasks finished after their due date lower your on-time rate, and every overdue open task takes 5 points off your output score.

Goals and progress

Set goals with your manager in Goals & Progress. Update the percentage with a short check-in each week. Completing a goal earns 25 points and appears on your profile.

Leave

  1. Go to Leave and choose the type: annual, sick, casual, maternity, paternity, study, compassionate, unpaid or other.
  2. Pick the dates and give a reason. Your remaining annual leave is shown.
  3. Your manager approves or declines; you're notified either way.

Recommended practice Request planned leave at least two weeks ahead. Report sick leave the same morning.

Expense claims

  1. Go to Expense Claims, enter the amount, date and category, and attach the receipt (photo or PDF).
  2. Finance approves or rejects it with notes (status: pending → approved or rejected).
  3. Admin marks approved claims reimbursed once paid.

Communication

  • Messages: direct and team chats. Every colleague card also has a WhatsApp button.
  • Meetings: schedule with colleagues; everyone gets a morning digest and a 15-minute reminder.
  • Office Feed: share wins, give kudos, comment and reshare. Announcements from management are pinned.
  • Social & Advocacy: share approved company posts on your own social accounts.

Payslips and profile

Your monthly payslips are in Payslips. Keep bank, emergency contact and personal details current in My Profile; only HR can see the private parts.

05 · Field work

Field work and verification

Anyone who visits clients, hospitals or partners checks in on the Staff Office. Each visit gets a trust score from evidence, so honest work is visible and can be defended.

Checking in at a client

  1. On arrival, open Field Visits → Check in at a client.
  2. Tap Share my location. Wait for the accuracy to improve (outdoors or near a window helps), or tap Use this location.
  3. Take a live photo of the reception, signboard or meeting. It's stamped with the time and location.
  4. Enter the organisation, purpose and contact person.
  5. Send the client confirmation link to the contact by WhatsApp or email. They confirm on their own phone, and can share their location.
  6. When you leave, check out with the outcome and the next step.

Location checks

System rule Staff clocked in on field work get one unannounced location check each weekday, at a random time between 10:30 and 15:30. Managers can also send one at any time. You have 20 minutes to share your location and a live photo. Missed checks notify your manager and lower your field score.

If your phone won't give a location: follow the steps it shows. The usual fixes are ending any phone or WhatsApp call, allowing location for your browser in phone Settings → Apps, turning on Google Location Accuracy, or opening Google Maps until the blue dot appears. If a help box shows a Details line, send a screenshot of it to your manager.

09 · Integrity

Integrity rules

Records in the Staff Office are used for pay, promotion and client trust. The following are treated as misconduct:

  • Using fake-GPS apps or anything that alters your location.
  • Confirming your own visit, or asking a friend or colleague to confirm it.
  • Reusing an old photo or uploading one instead of taking it live.
  • Checking in for someone else, or letting someone use your account.
  • Reporting visits, calls or sales that didn't happen.

Your privacy. Location is recorded only when you clock in or out, check in or out of a visit, or answer a location check. It's never tracked in the background. You can see everything recorded about you; your manager, HR and management can see it too.

Common questions

I forgot to clock out. What happens?

The day is closed automatically at your last activity and flagged. Tell your manager if the time is wrong.

My phone says "This site can't ask for your permission".

Another app is on top of the screen, often a WhatsApp call or chat bubble. End the call or close the bubble, or allow location and camera for your browser in phone Settings → Apps, then try again.

I'm office-based. Will field visits lower my score?

No. Field and sales scores only count for roles that use them or on days you record field work.

Who do I ask for help?

Your manager first, then HR. For account or sign-in problems, contact HR to reset your access.
