ORION MEDIA STUDIO — 20 OCTOBER 2026

WHERE TO START
Sign in to the website Admin. Open Blog, Social Media Content or Media Library.
The studio navigation lets you move between these three workspaces.

BLOG
1. Choose New article, or Use SchoolCore template.
2. Write the title and article. A summary and address are created automatically if left blank.
3. Add a cover picture, video or up to eight article pictures from the shared media library. Pictures have editable descriptions for accessibility.
4. Preview the article, then Save draft, Publish now or Schedule article.
Scheduled articles publish automatically on the server, normally within five minutes of the selected Lagos/WAT time. A browser does not need to remain open.
Publishing an article follows the Newsletter section's existing settings; existing subscriber notifications have not been switched on or off by this upgrade.
Unpublishing an existing article requires confirmation. Public articles use live metadata so edits do not require a new site build. Public-page metadata may take approximately 30 seconds to refresh at the hosting cache.
After publishing, Turn article into six social drafts carries the article title, summary and address into the campaign builder.

SOCIAL CAMPAIGNS
1. Choose SchoolCore starter or fill your own campaign brief.
2. Choose or upload the video/pictures once.
3. Press Prepare six channel drafts. This saves drafts for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok and YouTube.
4. Open each draft in Publication queue. Review its caption and media. Copy the caption, download the selected file and open your social account using its saved profile link.
5. Publish in the social platform. Paste its live post link into the studio and mark Published.
Due posts rise to the top of the queue. Filter by platform, status, campaign or caption.
Caption preparation uses editable templates and your factual brief, not an AI claim that it has watched/transcribed the video. Short X drafts use a shorter destination link and fewer hashtags to avoid breaking URLs.
Instagram/TikTok drafts use a profile/contact call to action; these accounts need their actual profile link set appropriately.

AUTOMATIONS INCLUDED
Shared file reuse, content-hash deduplication, video-cover generation where the browser supports it, file readability/type checks, upload progress, automatic article slugs and summaries, per-channel caption templates, tracking parameters where space allows, six-draft campaign creation, retry protection, unsaved-work recovery within the same browser tab, scheduled Blog publication and due-post sorting.
Tracking parameters require your website's analytics to report visits; generating them does not install analytics or promise attribution.
Publication dates for social posts currently organise the team queue; they do not send social posts automatically.

SOCIAL ACCOUNT CONNECTIONS
Settings social URLs are profile addresses, not API posting permissions. All six channels currently use the same manual handoff workflow. The studio shows this explicitly and does not claim that a scheduled draft has been sent.
Direct social publishing remains a separate activation/integration step requiring each network's authorised business/developer application, publishing permissions and sometimes a platform review or API plan. The existing Google sign-in credentials must not be mistaken for a YouTube upload grant.
No social password, token or payment-card detail belongs in a caption or this guide.
Platform requirements must be checked at activation. Examples of official documentation:

UPLOADS AND STORAGE
Supported files: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, MP4 and WebM; up to 100 MB each. Videos are checked for browser readability before upload. Export iPhone MOV/HEVC recordings as a compatible MP4 if necessary.
The library currently has a 2 GB application quota, plus checks for available disk space. It stores public marketing material on the existing persistent Railway volume, not in browser storage or as huge database values.
Files have unguessable content-hash addresses but are intended for public use; do not use this library for private staff records, meeting recordings or patient/student data.
The browser obtains a short-lived, single-use, file-specific upload permission and streams directly to the API host. No new storage provider, card entry or paid service was configured.
Video seeking uses HTTP range requests. Original files are retained; the library does not silently crop your videos or manufacture higher-resolution footage.

TEAM ACCESS
Owner, Editor and Client & Content Manager can use the publishing/media workflow. Financial, staff-office and admin-management restrictions remain enforced separately on the server.
Uploads/drafts are shared. Recovery copies are local to the same browser tab. Conflicting article/post saves are rejected rather than overwriting another editor's changes.

VERIFICATION
Isolated API tests cover permissions, persistent file writes, deduplication, content validation, single-use upload grants, range/HEAD responses, invalid ranges, unsafe origins, article schedules, concurrent-edit conflicts, campaign retry protection and publication-link validation.
Browser checks use isolated data at 1440, 820 and 390 pixels, including a content manager, actual video upload, cross-origin upload permission, automatic cover generation, article/video preview, failed-save recovery and six-channel campaign creation.
No real subscriber emails or social posts are sent by these tests. Real network account publishing has not been configured or tested.
